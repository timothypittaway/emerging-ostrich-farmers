# Interventions and development path

## The development path

The thesis proposed a path from subsistence to commercial farming, plotted against the combined score of the participation capacity instrument.

The **entry phase** is the hardest. Farmers must overcome dependence on mentors or government, limited knowledge of the supply chain, weak financial security, and limited training and market information. This is where government policy, black economic empowerment schemes (AgriBEE) and the private sector can do most to help programmes cross into commercial farming.

The Local Vulnerability Index gives a sense of how much input a location will need. The Agrarian Capacity and Market Access indices are where timely interventions can raise capacity fastest.

## Twelve recommended interventions

1. Carry out a feasibility study and review the location before starting a programme.
2. Provide support for programmes during start-up.
3. Improve how programme beneficiaries are selected.
4. Improve the structure of emerging farmers' co-operatives.
5. Provide ongoing, appropriate training in ostrich farming.
6. Define the roles of government and mentors clearly.
7. Reduce feed costs.
8. Link programmes to informal markets and encourage more home consumption.
9. Develop new export markets.
10. Provide alternative finance to cover the months with no income.
11. Open alternative marketing channels to export markets.
12. Give emerging farmers representation in the industry's leadership structures.

## Main constraints identified

- Limited farming knowledge and training in ostrich husbandry
- Weak leverage in the supply chain
- Poor selection of beneficiaries and conflict between participants
- No feasibility planning and weak economies of scale
- Dependence on mentors
- High feed costs
- High entry costs, biosecurity rules, market conditions and few marketing channels

## Areas for further research

- Cheaper methods of pre-heating ostrich meat for export
- Alternative feeds and lower feed costs
- Why the EU has imposed ostrich import bans
- Tourism opportunities for emerging farmers through arts and crafts
- Further testing of the participation instrument and its thresholds
- New markets for the South African ostrich industry
